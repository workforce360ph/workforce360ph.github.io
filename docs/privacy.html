Privacy & data handling

WorkForce360 is designed with privacy and security controls aligned with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Each client company remains the personal information controller and should complete its own compliance review with its Data Protection Officer before go-live.

Face detection and the liveness check run on the phone. WorkForce360 does not build or keep a face template (biometric profile) of any employee; only the selfie photo is saved with the punch, for the supervisor or HR to view if needed.

Lawful basis

The client company decides and documents its lawful basis. Typically, attendance and performance processing is needed to carry out the employment contract, to meet labor-law obligations on timekeeping and payroll records, and for the employer's legitimate interest in verifying attendance. Selfies and location are used only for attendance verification — not for monitoring outside working hours or for unrelated purposes.

Who can see what

  • Employees see only their own attendance, reports, scores and records.
  • Supervisors, OICs and managers see only the people who report to them.
  • HR, Admin and the Owner see company-wide data. Viewing a selfie or location proof is written to the audit log.
  • Only work emails added and approved by HR or Admin can sign in. Removing access signs the person out right away.

Employee rights and requests

  • A privacy notice is shown before an employee's first clock-in, and they must acknowledge it.
  • Employees can ask HR to access, correct or delete their data, or to object to processing, as allowed by law.
  • Attendance corrections go through an in-app request with approval and are logged.
  • HR can export an employee's records as CSV and signed coaching forms as PDF.

Deletion procedure

  1. The employee or the company sends a written request to HR.
  2. HR checks whether any record must be kept longer under labor law or a pending case.
  3. Data not required to be kept is deleted from the system and backups within the agreed period; the deletion is written to the audit log.
  4. HR confirms the deletion to the requester.

Security

  • Sign-in with a one-time code sent to the approved work email.
  • Role-based access and a full audit trail.
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS). The live version adds encrypted storage and daily backups.
  • Data belongs to the client company and can be exported at any time.
Note: This page describes how WorkForce360 is designed. It is not legal advice. Each company should review it with its Data Protection Officer and adjust its privacy notice, retention periods and lawful basis before go-live.
