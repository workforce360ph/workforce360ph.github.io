FAQ

Questions owners ask

Can employees fake their attendance?

It's much harder than with logbooks or a plain app. A live selfie needs head movement, so a printed photo won't pass. GPS must be inside the employee's branch, and the QR option uses a code that changes every minute. Anything unusual is flagged for the manager instead of silently accepted.

Does it work with weak or no signal?

Yes. The selfie, liveness check, GPS and QR scan all run on the phone itself, so employees can clock in without internet. In the live version, punches made offline are saved on the phone and sync automatically when the signal returns, marked "Recorded offline" for review.

We have several branches. Will staff at other branches show as absent or "away"?

No. Each branch has its own location and radius, and each employee is checked against their home branch. Roving staff can be allowed at any branch.

Is it compliant with the Data Privacy Act?

WorkForce360 is designed with privacy and security controls aligned with the Philippine Data Privacy Act, subject to each company's own compliance review. Location and selfie are captured only at clock-in and clock-out, employees acknowledge a privacy notice, selfies are deleted automatically after 90 days, access is by role, and an audit trail records who viewed what. See Privacy & data handling for what we collect, why, how long it's kept and how deletion works.

Does it compute payroll?

No — it prepares the attendance side of payroll. For each cutoff it totals hours worked, late minutes, undertime, overtime (computed and approved), leave, rest days worked and regular or special holiday hours per employee, and exports it to Excel. Your payroll system or accountant applies pay rates, premiums and deductions.

How is an employee's score calculated?

The overall score combines attendance, KPI achievement and tasks done on time, each with a weight the company sets (for example 30% · 40% · 30%). KPI achievement is each KPI's actual vs target times its weight, capped at 120%. The band thresholds and evaluation period (monthly or quarterly) are set by HR or Admin, and every employee can see the full breakdown of their own score.

How do daily sales reports work?

Before going home, each employee lists their sales or orders — OR number (optional), units, description and amount — and the app adds up the totals. Supervisors, OICs and managers send a weekly team report with units and sales per day, filled in from those daily reports. HR and Admin can add the columns or questions your business needs, and everything downloads to Excel.

Who can sign in to the app?

Only employees whose work email HR has added and HR or Admin has approved. They sign in with a one-time code sent to that email. Anyone else can only send an access request, and removing someone's access signs them out.

Can we use our own KPIs, shifts and logo?

Yes. KPIs, weights, targets, shifts, holidays, branches, leave credits, colors and company name are all set in Settings — no redesign needed.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, through the web app in Safari — add it to the home screen and it opens like an app. The Android app is available now.

How long does setup take?

About 7 weeks to go fully live: a pilot with one team first, then performance features, then all branches.
