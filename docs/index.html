FAQ

Questions owners ask

Can employees fake their attendance?

It's much harder than with logbooks or a plain app. A live selfie needs head movement, so a printed photo won't pass. GPS must be inside the employee's branch, and the QR option uses a code that changes every minute. Anything unusual is flagged for the manager instead of silently accepted.

Does it work with weak or no signal?

Yes. The selfie, liveness check, GPS and QR scan all run on the phone itself, so employees can clock in without internet. In the live version, punches made offline are saved on the phone and sync automatically when the signal returns, marked "Recorded offline" for review.

We have several branches. Will staff at other branches show as absent or "away"?

No. Each branch has its own location and radius, and each employee is checked against their home branch. Roving staff can be allowed at any branch.

Is it compliant with the Data Privacy Act?

It's designed for it: location and photo are captured only at clock-in, employees see a privacy notice, selfies are deleted automatically, access is by role, and an audit trail records who viewed what. We recommend a review with your privacy officer before go-live.

Can we use our own KPIs, shifts and logo?

Yes. KPIs, weights, targets, shifts, holidays, branches, leave credits, colors and company name are all set in Settings — no redesign needed.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, through the web app in Safari — add it to the home screen and it opens like an app. The Android app is available now.

How long does setup take?

About 7 weeks to go fully live: a pilot with one team first, then performance features, then all branches.
